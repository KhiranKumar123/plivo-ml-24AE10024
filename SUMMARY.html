Research & Methodology

We designed and executed an advanced two-stage gradient-free optimization process over the style-tensor space of the frozen Kokoro-82M model to clone the target speaker's voice qualities:

  • Stage 1: Convex Mixture Search - Instead of searching the raw $510 \times 256$ tensor space, we first optimize a convex combination of the top 10 stock voices. This shrinks the parameters to just 10 mixture weights (softmax-transformed to satisfy constraints), forcing the optimized voice to stay in the natural manifold of clear, intelligible speech.
  • Stage 2: Global Perturbation Search - Starting from the best convex blend, we perform local perturbations. Crucially, we repeat a single $1 \times 256$ perturbation vector across all 510 rows. This structured perturbation shifts the entire voice space uniformly, preventing row-level distortion and ensuring the voice generalises perfectly to unseen sentences.
